# 2026.243.1021 — chore: advance the pinned plugin-wl to the exec and clipboard fixes

## Summary
    require:  @github.com/opencharly/plugin-wl/candy/plugin-wl
              v2026.242.2204 -> v2026.243.1001

plugin-wl#9 fixed two defects of one class: wl exec and wl clipboard set BLOCKED for their
whole deadline on commands that do not exit. exec backgrounded the job but the child inherited
stdout and stderr, so the capture pipe never reached EOF; wl-copy stays alive to own the
Wayland selection and did the same. Both reported failure for operations that had succeeded -
a GUI app that launched and mapped, a clipboard that was set.

This candy is the nested-compositor primitive, so every consumer of it drives apps through
exactly those verbs. Until this pin moves they get the blocking versions.

First of four ordered repins. The other three are the paths from distro-omarchy's
omarchy-cstream box down to this candy:

    box/omarchy-cstream -> layer-cstream-desktop   -> pod-hyprland (HERE) -> plugin-wl
                        -> layer-omarchy-hyprland  -> pod-hyprland (HERE) -> plugin-wl

Both paths have to arrive. This exact chain was walked earlier for v2026.242.2204, and the
lesson it taught is why all four land together rather than one: advancing a consumer WITHOUT
the chain reopens the version split. That is what happened here - the omarchy desktop bed
moved to v2026.243.1001 for the fixes and charly immediately warned that plugin-wl resolved to
two versions, newest 2026.243.0512 from v2026.243.1001, ignoring 2026.242.2239 from
v2026.242.2204.
