# 2026.242.0634 — feat: repin plugin-wl to the fixed Hyprland dispatchers

## Summary

Advance the pinned `plugin-wl` from `v2026.240.1326` to `v2026.242.0610`.

**This candy is where the cstream bed resolves the `wl:` verb**, so the fix in that tag reaches
nothing until this pin moves. What it carries:

- **`wl: close` and `wl: fullscreen` were acting on the FOCUSED window, not the named one.** Every
  `hl.dsp.window.*` dispatcher operates on the active window, and the ones that accept a selector
  ignore it while still returning `ok` — so the action reported success against a window it never
  touched. Measured on 0.56.2 with four windows mapped: focused C, asked it to close D, **C died
  and D survived**.
- **`wl: focus` and `wl: minimize` are implemented**, where they previously errored as unsupported
  on Hyprland.

## Change classification

**Change class:** candy/box-config — one pinned ref, one candy `version:`. No Go, no schema, no
workflow, single repo.

**Verification gate:** `check-cstream-pod`, the bed that composes this candy and drives the `wl:`
verb through it.

**Breaking change + rollback:** `wl: close` and `wl: fullscreen` change which window they act on —
**to the correct one**. A consumer depending on the old behaviour was depending on a bug. Rollback
is a plain revert.

**Producer → consumer order:** satisfied — `plugin-wl#6` is merged and `v2026.242.0610` is
published.
